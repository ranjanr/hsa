Balanced Housing Stability & Legal Compliance

A mobile-first AI assistant for California tenants and small landlords. Navigate eviction notices, calculate lawful rent caps, generate CA lease agreements, and draft formal legal letters.

🛡️ For Tenants

  • Eviction Notice Audit: Scan notices for illegal flaws and exact response deadlines.
  • Rent Increase Validator: Calculate AB 1482 (8.8%) and San Jose ARO (5%) legal caps.
  • Repair Request Generator: Auto-draft formal habitability letters under CA Civil Code § 1941.1.
  • Tenancy Evidence Log: Maintain a timestamped chronological log for legal aid defense.

⚖️ For Small Landlords

  • CA Lease Agreement Generator: Create full leases with AB 1482 & AB 12 deposit limit disclosures.
  • Notice Compliance Checker: Ensure written notices meet mandatory statutory language.
  • Rent Cap Calculator: Verify lawful annual rent adjustments before serving notices.
  • 24-Hour Entry Notices: Draft legally compliant property entry reminders under Civ. Code § 1954.
